Frequently asked questions

What video formats do you support?

We support MP4, MOV, and WebM files up to 200MB or 10 minutes in length.

Do you support languages other than English?

Yes, our AI transcription engine supports over 90 languages including Spanish, French, Hindi, and Japanese.

Can I use my own custom fonts?

Custom font upload is available on our Pro plan. For Creator users, we provide a curated selection of 20+ premium fonts.
