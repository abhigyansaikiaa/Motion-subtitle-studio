Koala

The koala
(Phascolarctos cinereus) is an arboreal, herbivorous marsupial native to Australia. Often inaccurately called a "koala bear," it is actually a marsupial that carries its developing young in a pouch.

Physical Traits
Koalas have stout, tailless bodies covered in thick ash-grey fur with cream-coloured chests. They possess large, round ears, a prominent black leathery nose, and long, muscular limbs equipped with sharp claws designed for climbing.

Conservation Status:
Koalas face severe threats from habitat loss, urbanization, climate change, bushfires, and diseases like Chlamydia. In February 2022, the Australian government officially listed the koala as Endangered across Queensland, New South Wales, and the Australian Capital Territory.
